Vaccine Needle Depth Guide

Most vaccines are meant to go into muscle. If the needle stops too short, the dose can end up in fat. If it goes too deep or too high on the shoulder, it can irritate the joint lining or bone. This guide helps you talk with the person giving your shot about needle length, skin technique, and arm position.

For discussion and education only. This is not medical advice. The person giving your vaccine will choose the needle and technique based on what they see and feel.
